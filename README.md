# 🌸 My Favorite Series: *Dolce Amore*

![Dolce Amore Banner](https://i.mydramalist.com/0wKAAk_3f.jpg)

## 📖 Overview

**Dolce Amore** is a **Filipino romantic drama television series** that aired in 2016.  
It stars **Liza Soberano** and **Enrique Gil**, and tells the story of two people from different worlds who find love despite the distance, culture, and life circumstances that separate them.

## 🎬 Main Characters

| Character | Actor/Actress | Description |
|------------|----------------|--------------|
| Serena Marchesa | Liza Soberano | A Filipina adopted by an Italian family who searches for her roots. |
| Tenten Ibarra | Enrique Gil | A hardworking young man from Tondo who dreams of a better life. |
| Luciana Marchesa | Cherie Gil | Serena’s adoptive mother who hides a secret. |

---

## 🧭 Plot Summary

1. **Serena**, raised in Italy, feels out of place and curious about her Filipino heritage.  
2. **Tenten**, an orphan from Tondo, works multiple jobs to support his adoptive family.  
3. Fate brings them together through a series of heartwarming and dramatic twists.  
4. Their love is tested by *family secrets, social class, and destiny.*

---

## 💕 Why I Love It

- The mix of **Italian and Filipino culture** is beautifully portrayed 🇮🇹🇵🇭  
- Amazing **chemistry** between the lead actors.  
- Inspiring story about **identity, love, and family.**  
- Great soundtrack (especially *“Your Love* by Juris Fernandez 🎵)
